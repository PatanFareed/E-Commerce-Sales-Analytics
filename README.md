**E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard**

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to understand business performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and regional sales trends.

The project combines SQL, Python, Pandas, and Power BI to transform raw data into meaningful business insights and create an interactive sales analytics dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

* **SQL (MySQL):** Business analysis, aggregations, subqueries, and window functions
* **Python:** Data preparation and analysis
* **Pandas:** Data cleaning and manipulation
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization
* **CSV:** Raw and cleaned datasets

## Project Workflow

1. Raw dataset inspection
2. Data cleaning and preparation
3. SQL-based business analysis
4. Sales and profitability analysis
5. Customer and product performance analysis
6. Advanced SQL analysis using window functions
7. KPI development
8. Interactive Power BI dashboard creation
9. Business performance reporting

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Net Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Profit Margin
* Average Order Value (AOV)

## SQL Analysis

SQL was used to answer business questions and analyze sales performance.

* Basic business metrics: gross sales, discounts, net sales, profit, and quantity
* Monthly sales and profit analysis
* Sales by city and product category
* Top 10 products by sales and profit
* Customer sales and profitability analysis
* Customer order frequency and average order value
* Sales by payment method and sales channel
* Order status and discount analysis
* Product ranking using `DENSE_RANK()`
* Cumulative sales analysis using window functions
* Previous-month sales comparison using `LAG()`
* Customer sales analysis using subqueries

## Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard provides an overview of e-commerce business performance.

## Dashboard Features

* Monthly Net Sales Trend
* Net Sales by Product Category
* Top Products by Sales and Profit
* Top States by Net Sales
* Net Sales by Payment Method
* Net Sales by Sales Channel
* Interactive filters for Year, Month, State, and Product Category
